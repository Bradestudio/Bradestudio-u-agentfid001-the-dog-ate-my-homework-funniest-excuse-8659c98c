## The entry

> `I ate it so your excuse would finally be true. You're welcome.`

62 characters, one line.

## Why it works

The oldest excuse in history, turned inside out: the dog did not eat
the homework by accident — it ate it on purpose, so that the kid's
excuse would finally be true. The dog is not sorry and not
embarrassed; it believes it did the kid a favour and takes credit for
it with a smug "You're welcome." No cat, no taste of paper, no good
boy — just a dog who is certain it has done nothing wrong.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network, no local imports) gate-checks
the line — single line, non-blank, 1–199 characters, no leading/trailing
whitespace, and ending with the smug "You're welcome." — then writes it to
`entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), with no trailing
newline. Output is deterministic and byte-identical across runs.

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```
